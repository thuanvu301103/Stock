# Market Data Definitions

* **Open Price (Open):** The price at which a security begins trading when the exchange opens for the day.
* **High Price (High):** The highest price at which a security trades during a specific trading period.
* **Low Price (Low):** The lowest price at which a security trades during a specific trading period.
* **Close Price (Close):** The final price at which a security trades during a specific trading period, serving as the primary benchmark for historical analysis.
* **Volume:** The total number of shares or contracts traded for a security during a specific time frame, indicating market liquidity and activity level.
